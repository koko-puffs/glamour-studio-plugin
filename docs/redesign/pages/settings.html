Settings: plain and quiet

As the site's account and settings pages: one column (640 wide, as Housing Studio's and Raid Planner's plugins), General and Account as a segmented pick, each group a quiet label over a card of rows, a switch or a button at each row's end. No field, no holo, and a sticker only where linking is the thing to do. Every setting is today's; the recorder lives at the foot of General.
