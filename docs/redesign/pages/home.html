  1. 1The first run is the field's moment. LINK YOUR GAME (the site's own words for it) on the field, the three steps as ink callouts side by side, unchanged from today's Get started.
  2. 2Link your account is the sticker. It opens Settings → Account with the token field focused. Open Glamour Studio opens the site in your browser.
  3. 3Empty pages are drawn, as the site's are. The sign: a white card in display capitals with a few hand-lettered words under them, turned 5° (the vertices rotated after drawing) with a hard deep-violet block; a cloud drifting at its foot (a texture, tinted). No hand holds it.

  5. 5The pill says you're not linked, a faint dot, your character's initials on the soft ink rather than the field's violet. Not logged in at all: a ? in the face and “Log in to a character”.
