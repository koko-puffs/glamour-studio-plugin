  1. 1Toasts: one at a time, a pill of raised ink rising at the window's foot (or the panel's, when the window's closed), gone in four seconds. Good news starts with the holo sparkle; a warning or a failure is amber with a warning sign, never the sparkle (a failure mustn't twinkle).
  2. 2Tooltips: the same raised ink, a strong hairline, 10px corners, 12.5px Figtree; a name in semibold, amber for what to see to. A disabled button's tooltip says why.
  3. 3Combos and menus are ImGui's own, restyled: the pill opens a popup of raised ink with 16px corners, group names as faint spaced capitals, codes in Geist Mono, the pick lilac-washed with a tick.
  4. 4The pill's states, the first that's true winning, as today's status pill: recording, the plate editor, not linked, a refused token, sending, a failed send, a character not asked yet or said no, then sent and when.
  5. 5A screen that failed and Refresh while reading: small, honest, and the rest keeps working.
