Beside the game's windows: the dresser and its plates

A small panel in the plugin's look sits on the edge of the game window you have open, moving with it, as tall as what's in it. Its header is the same everywhere: the clothes tag, GLAMOUR STUDIO over what it's beside in heavy capitals, then Refresh, Open Glamour Studio and the fold. The game's windows here are only their outlines: they are the game's.
